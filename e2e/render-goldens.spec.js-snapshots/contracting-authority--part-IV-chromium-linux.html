The economic operator will allow checks to be conducted on the production capacities or the technical capacity of the economic operator and, where necessary, on the means of study and research which are available to it as well as on the quality control measures. The checks are required in the case where complex products or services are to be supplied or, exceptionally, for products or services which are required for a special purpose. The checks are to be performed by the buyer or a competent official body designated by the buyer, coming from the country in which the supplier or service provider is established.
