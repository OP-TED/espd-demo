Part IV: Selection criteria

Criteria regarding suitability, economic/financial standing, technical/professional ability, and quality/environmental management

A: Suitability

The economic operator is enrolled in the relevant professional registers kept in the Member State of its establishment as described in the relevant legislation.

The economic operator is enrolled in trade registers kept in the Member State of its establishment as described in the relevant legislation.

The economic operator has the necessary membership or authorisation of a particular organisation, to be able to perform the service in the country of its establishment.

B: Economic and financial standing

The general yearly turnover for the required number of financial years of the economic operator.

The average yearly turnover for the required number of financial years of the economic operator.

A specific average yearly turnover in the business area covered by the contract for the required number of years is required of the economic operator.

A specific yearly turnover in the business area covered by the procurement concerned for the required number of financial years is required of the economic operator.

The actual values for the required financial ratios of the economic operator.

A professional risk indemnity insurance is required for the performance on this contract.

Other economic or financial requirements of the economic operator.

C: Technical and professional ability

References for the specified works, during the specified period, performed by the economic operator. Buyers may require up to five years’ experience and allow experience dating for more than five years.

References for the specified deliveries, during the specified period, performed by the economic operator. Buyers may require up to three years’ experience and allow experience dating from more than three years for supply contracts.

References for the specified services, during the specified period, performed by the economic operator. Buyers may require up to three years’ experience and allow experience dating from more than three years.

The economic operator employs technicians or technical bodies, especially those responsible for quality control.

The economic operator employs technicians or technical bodies to carry out the work, in the case of works contracts.

The economic operator can put measures in place for ensuring quality.

The economic operator can use study, technical and research facilities.

The economic operator can apply supply chain management and tracking systems.

The economic operator can apply environmental management measures.

The economic operator can use tools, plant, or technical equipment.

The economic operator has the relevant educational and professional qualifications.

The economic operator will allow checks to be conducted on the production capacities or the technical capacity of the economic operator and, where necessary, on the means of study and research which are available to it as well as on the quality control measures. The checks are required in the case where complex products or services are to be supplied or, exceptionally, for products or services which are required for a special purpose. The checks are to be performed by the buyer or a competent official body designated by the buyer, coming from the country in which the supplier or service provider is established.

The number of managerial staff of the economic operator for the last three years.

The average yearly manpower of the economic operator for the last three years.

The economic operator intends possibly to subcontract a proportion of the contract.

The economic operator can supply the required samples, descriptions, or photographs of the products to be supplied, which do not need to be accompanied by certifications of authenticity.

The economic operator can supply the required samples, descriptions, or photographs of the products to be supplied and to provide certifications of authenticity.

The economic operator can provide the required certificates drawn up by official quality control institutes or agencies of recognised competence attesting the conformity of products clearly identified by references to the technical specifications or standards.

D: Quality assurance schemes and environmental management standards

The economic operator can produce certificates drawn up by independent bodies attesting that the economic operator complies with the required quality assurance standards, including accessibility for disabled persons.

The economic operator can produce certificates drawn up by independent bodies attesting that the economic operator complies with the required environmental management systems or standards.
